## Booking

No single site wins every time. Search in two layers: pull a baseline from AutoSlash, then cross-check the same two or three channels.

- [AutoSlash](https://www.autoslash.com/): the baseline. It runs the search against hundreds of coupon codes, memberships (AAA, AARP, Costco) and corporate codes at once instead of just comparing list prices. Fill in the memberships once and the profile sticks.
- [Costco Travel](https://www.costcotravel.com/Rental-Cars): Slick deal for certain rental agencies, currently Alamo, Avis, Budget, Enterprise. Comes with one free additional driver, and some Costco cards give extra points. Costco membership required. US works better.
- Card travel portals ([Chase Travel](https://travel.chase.com/), [Citi Travel](https://www.citi.com/credit-cards/citi-travel), [Capital One Travel](https://travel.capitalone.com/)): pricing varies a lot between them. No need to open all three, just the one or two where you hold the card and can pay with points or earn a multiplier.
- Booking direct with a discount code: every brand has a code field (AWD for Avis, BCD for Budget, CDP for Hertz) that takes AAA, AARP, Costco or issuer codes. A discount code stacks with one coupon code, but only one discount code at a time.
- Cashback portals (Capital One Shopping, Rakuten): occasionally 30-45% back at Hertz or Sixt. When one shows up, clicking through and booking direct often beats every aggregator.

:::warning

Easy things to miss while comparing:

- Don't book prepaid. Rates swing by tens of dollars within a day, and a non-refundable rate gives up every drop that follows. Book pay-at-pickup and let AutoSlash track it, then rebook when it falls. It tracks reservations booked elsewhere too.
- Portals usually can't carry a loyalty number (Hertz Gold, Avis Preferred), which means the counter line. On a late arrival, paying a bit more to book direct can be worth it.
- Damage waiver is a hidden cost. Some cards are secondary only inside the US, so compare the price with coverage in mind.

:::

## 保險

大致上有這幾種保險，主要需要保的是前兩種，其他兩種只要不要耍笨基本上用不到：）

- Damage Waiver: 車體險；不用負擔自己開的車的損壞費用
- Liability Protection: 第三人責任險；不用負擔被你撞爛的車 (或人) 的費用
- Roadside Assistance : 道路救援；拋錨、爆胎、沒油、鑰匙鎖車等等費用
- Personal Effects: 個人財物險；保險公司賠償在車內被偷的財物

:::tip

大部分信用卡會有包含 DW，但是會有額外的條款和限制，像是

- Primary or Secondary Coverage
  - Primary: 信用卡負責全部的費用
  - Secondary: 信用卡只負責超過租車公司保險的部分
- 租車天數 (通常不超過 30 天)
- 駕駛地區
- 出險金額上限

另外，如果你本來就有車險，它們也有可能本來就涵蓋了租車的保險。

如果這些其他保險涵蓋的範圍夠用，也許可以省下一筆錢 ✨

:::

## 可能的額外費用

- Additional Driver Fee: 額外駕駛費用
- Young Driver Fee: 未滿 25 歲的額外費用
- Toll fee: 通行費，通常自己用車牌號碼申請 Toll pass 會比較便宜，可以省下租車公司多收的手續費，但在某些州自己搞可能會是一件麻煩的事情
- Skip pump fee: 不加油還車，租車公司通常會按照比較貴的油價收費
- SiriusXM®: 衛星電台，沒用過但聽起來廢到笑

## Tips

- 領車：檢查油箱、問要加哪種油、拍照錄影 (有保 DW 的話可以跳過)
- 下車：把個人物品放後車廂或看不到的地方，避免不必要的麻煩
- 出事：報案留底，不然保險可能不理賠
- 攔停：警察如果問「你知道你為什麼被攔下來」，永遠回答「不知道」
- 還車：記得先加油
