# 📧 Email

How to get an email address, and features you might want to take advantage of.

## Services

| Service                                 | Start At         | Catch-All      | Custom Domain              | Hide Address            |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------- | -------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| [Fastmail](https://www.fastmail.com/)   | $5 / mo          | ✅             | 1 address, 600 alias       | ✅ Masked Email         |
| [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/mail)   | Free, $3.99 / mo | ✅ paid        | 1 address, 10 alias        | ✅ hide-my-email alias  |
| [Tuta](https://tuta.com/)               | Free, €3 / mo    | ✅ paid        | 1 address, unlimited alias | ❌                      |
| [Mailfence](https://mailfence.com/)     | Free, €2.50 / mo | ✅ Entry €3.50 | 3 address, 50 alias        | ❌                      |
| [Posteo](https://posteo.de/)            | €1 / mo          | ❌             | ❌ by design               | ❌ 3 fixed aliases      |
| [Mailbox.org](https://mailbox.org/)     | €1 / mo          | ✅ Standard €3 | 1 address, 50 alias        | ❌                      |
| [Runbox](https://runbox.com/)           | €19.95 / yr      | ✅             | 1 address, unlimited alias | ❌                      |
| [StartMail](https://www.startmail.com/) | $4.99 / mo       | ✅             | 1 address, unlimited alias | ✅ unlimited disposable |
| [CounterMail](https://countermail.com/) | $4 / mo          |                | 1 address, unlimited alias | ✅ dynamic alias        |
| [Disroot](https://disroot.org/)         | Free (donation)  | ✅             | 1 address                  | ❌                      |
| [MXroute](https://mxroute.com/)         | $59 / yr         | ✅             | Unlimited address + alias  | ❌                      |

Prices are per user on annual billing. Counts are for the cheapest tier that supports custom
domains. An address is one you own and can send from, an alias is an extra address that delivers
into it. Entry tiers are single user apart from MXroute, which hosts unlimited domains and accounts
on one subscription.

## Catch-All

Bring your own domain, setup on these services, all emals to `*@yourdomain.com` will be forwarded to your inbox.

- [Fastmail](https://www.fastmail.help/hc/en-us/articles/1500000277942-Catch-all-wildcard-aliases)
- [Cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-routing/get-started/enable-email-routing/)
- [Mailcast](https://mailcast.io/)
- [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/support/knowledgebase/article.aspx/310/2214/how-to-set-up-a-catchall-wildcard-email-address/)
- [Name.com](https://www.name.com/support/articles/360016305593-customizing-your-name-com-email-spam-settings-and-allowed-senders-list)
- [NameSilo](https://www.namesilo.com/support/v2/articles/email/email-forwarding)

## Hiding / Relay

By hiding your email address with these services, websites and apps will not be able to know your real email address.

- [iCloud Hide My Email](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/set-up-hide-my-email-mm9d9012c9e8/icloud)
- [addy.io](https://addy.io/)
- [Firefox Relay](https://relay.firefox.com/)
- [SimpleLogin](https://simplelogin.io/)
